### Interview Preparation

| Task | What I'll Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Research common interview questions | Collect the questions Report Writer / Technical Writer interviewers typically ask | 1 hr |
| Prepare answers using STAR | Structure my answers to behavioural questions as Situation, Task, Action, Result | 2 hrs |
| Practice explaining my project work | Rehearse describing my GitHub repo, README, and documentation examples from this course | 1 hr |
| Prepare questions to ask the interviewer | Write 3-5 questions about the team, documentation tools, and review process | 30 min |
| Do a mock interview | Practice out loud with a friend, teammate, or by recording myself | 1 hr |
| Review and refine | Note what felt weak and practice those answers again | 1 hr |

**Question Areas I'm Preparing For**

- How I'd explain a technical topic to someone with no background in it
- How I handle it when a subject-matter expert is slow to respond or gives incomplete information
- My process for researching and structuring a new document from scratch
- An example of documentation I've improved or reorganised for clarity
- How I prioritise when I have multiple documents due around the same time
- How I handle receiving critical feedback on something I've written
- What tools I use for writing, formatting, and version control, and why
- My definition of what makes documentation genuinely effective
